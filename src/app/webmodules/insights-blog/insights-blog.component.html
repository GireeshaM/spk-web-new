Salesforce Health Cloud is an IT CRM system for the healthcare industry that includes services for managing doctor-patient relationships and records. Through a patient profile that incorporates information from different data sources, such as electronic medical records (EMRs), healthcare equipment, and wearables, Health Cloud enables one-to-one relationship management.

Patients can also use the system from anywhere they want, anytime and from any gadget to reach out and interact with staff, find solutions to the FAQs, complete documentation before their visit, and do other things.

This platform safeguards every data it controls, making its use completely secure and dependable. The Health Cloud allows users and healthcare providers to view every patient record in actual time, including healthcare records and scheduled health check-up appointments.

Salesforce developed a platform that provides a 360-degree view of the connection between patients, clinicians, and healthcare institutions by utilizing advanced CRM (Customer Relationship Management) technology. In one integrated system, healthcare records, previous medications, wellness programs, exams, and medicines are all able to be accessed, organized, and managed.

As a result, conventional health information generated on paper and maintained in documents can be readily substituted with an intelligent solution that is legally approved and incorporated into every facet of medical treatment.

What is available with the Salesforce Health Cloud?
In addition to coordinating post-acute care, creating care plans, and scaling care management, the Health Cloud can monitor patient itineraries and identify treatment gaps. Patient data is safely and conveniently connected to onboarding and acquisition procedures through various channels. Salesforce Health Cloud can be used to track advancements in patient care by providing personalized information and pertinent messages to track a patient's possible reaction to various drugs. Records are not the focus of the Health Cloud; rather, interactions are.

The Salesforce Health Cloud makes it possible to do things like:
